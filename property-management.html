The approach

How it works

Each property is run like a small hotel room: cleaned between stays, priced to demand and supported by one contact for the owner.

The arrangement depends on how involved you want to be.

Working with us

Two ways to work with us

The service is the same in both. The difference is whether your income varies with bookings or is fixed.

01

Full Management

We price to local demand, take bookings, look after guests and arrange cleaning and repairs.

You receive booking income less a management fee, with a monthly report on occupancy, nightly rate and revenue.

02

Rent Guarantee

You receive the same agreed amount each month. We cover marketing, running costs and day-to-day care.

Predictable income with nothing to manage.
