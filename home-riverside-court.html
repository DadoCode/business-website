The apartment

Private balcony, room to settle in

A light-filled two bedroom apartment on the fourth floor of a modern riverside building, with a private balcony and an open-plan living and dining room.

Battersea Park station is a short walk away, with fast connections to Victoria and the West End, and the park and river path on the doorstep.
